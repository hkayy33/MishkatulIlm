About Us

At Al Usooliyyah Academy, we believe Arabic is more than a means of communication—it is the language of the Holy Quran and a gateway to a rich intellectual and cultural heritage.

Our journey began with a simple vision: to provide authentic Arabic education built on clear understanding, structured learning, and effective teaching methods. Over time, this vision grew into a range of programs designed to help students develop both proficiency and a meaningful connection with the Arabic language.

Today, Al Usooliyyah Academy serves as a bridge for learners around the world, connecting them to the language of the Quran in a supportive educational environment founded on knowledge, excellence, and care for every student.

Meet the Founder

Al Usooliyyah Academy was founded by Al Ustaadh Ibraheem, a professional and certified Arabic language and Qur’an tutor who is passionate about teaching and impacting others with my knowledge. I hold a diploma in the Arabic language and Islamic studies from the Great Arabic Institute of Nigeria in affiliation with Islamic University of Madina, Saudi Arabia. I also completed and passed the course entitled How to Teach Arabic and Islamic Studies (An Introduction to Language Teaching Methodology), along with teaching certification in the Arabic language for both native speakers and non-native speakers of all ages and skill levels.

With over 8 years of teaching experience, both online tutoring and face-to-face, I recently had the incredible opportunity to collaborate with esteemed scholars from Saudi Arabia who hold appointments in Arabic schools. Their unique perspectives and innovative approaches truly enriched my teaching style. Engaging with them opened my eyes to new methodologies and cultural insights.

I memorized the Quran completely and obtained ijaazah (certification) in Qira'at and other Islamic books and books of Tajweed.
